About the server

BorderlandsSMP is a modded Minecraft survival server set on a completely custom map, so this is a world you won't find anywhere else. With more than 100 mods built around Create, you can run working factories, fly your own airships, lay railways and fire heavy cannons.

We also use a canon player system, so what you do here becomes part of the server's story.

The vibe is part engineering, part adventure, with a community of players from all around the world. Our Discord is the place to say hello and find out how to join.
